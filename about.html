About Us

One company, built to solve any IT challenge — with AI at the core

Mindful Tech Solutions is the parent company behind a growing family of AI-native products, alongside a full-service custom IT practice.

We started as a custom IT solutions firm — solving one-off problems for clients who needed software nobody else would build for them. Along the way, we noticed the same patterns showing up across industries: manual processes, spreadsheet sprawl, and workflows held together by institutional memory.

Rather than keep solving the same problem privately for every client, we started building products. RestOps 360, Proforma OS, and Crew OS are the result — AI-native platforms born directly out of real operational pain we saw again and again.

Today, Mindful Tech Solutions operates as both a product company and a custom solutions partner. Whatever the size of the problem, we bring the same AI-first engineering discipline to it.
